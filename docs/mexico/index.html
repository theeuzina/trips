The experience

A plan with room for life to happen.

This isn't six days following someone holding an umbrella. And it isn't six days figuring everything out yourself.

Every day has somewhere worth going. What happens around it has room to breathe. Breakfast might turn into another coffee. A neighborhood might lead us to a taco stand. Dinner might turn into drinks, and drinks might turn into dancing.

The structure is there. So is the freedom.

The table

Come hungry.

Mexico City is a city you experience through food.

75 restaurants recognized by the Michelin Guide. 11 with Michelin stars. And yes — one of them is a taquería.

Markets. Mole. Masa. Chocolate. Street food. Long dinners. Mezcal.

One day might mean a restaurant worth crossing the city for. The next, tacos from somewhere we smelled half a block away. We're going to eat well. Very well.

After dark

Stay out.

Mexico City doesn't call it a night after dinner. Neither do we.

One evening might mean a rooftop overlooking the city. Another, a neighborhood cantina built for sitting around longer than you intended. Another might start with tacos and end with cocktails somewhere completely different.

The conversation. The music. The people you meet. The night is part of the trip.
