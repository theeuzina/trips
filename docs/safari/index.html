Retreat Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Traditional Safari Arrival

    Welcome to Amaya Lodge! Settle into your luxurious tented suite before embarking on an exhilarating afternoon game drive, culminating in sunset drinks.

    As evening falls, join us for a fireside welcome dinner and the retreat's opening circle.

  2. Day 2: Conservation Experience

    Track rhinos and explore their world through the eyes of those protecting them, gaining rare insight into one of Africa’s most pressing conservation efforts. The afternoon includes a dedicated conservation workshop with our experts, followed by time in the nearby town of Hoedspruit to experience local culture, community life, and the spirit of the region—ending with dinner at Amaya.

  3. Day 3: Walking Safari & Wild Picnic

    Immerse yourself in the bush on a morning guided walk with our expert rangers, learning to track wildlife and interpret the subtle language of the land. The journey leads to a remote riverbed for a mindful picnic and yoga session in the wild, where stillness meets nature in its purest form. The day concludes with an evening game drive and a reflective fireside dinner under the stars.

  4. Day 4: Further Immersion & Exploration

    Enjoy a leisurely morning game drive. A midday wellness session is also available. In the evening, enjoy a traditional South African dinner under the stars, offering a moment for personal reflection and connection with nature.

  5. Day 5: Final Full Day & Farewell

    Savour one last morning game drive. Enjoy a midday wellness session or spend the afternoon at leisure enjoying the lodge's amenities. In the evening, join us for a final evening game drive which concludes in a special farewell fireside dinner to celebrate your journey.

  6. Day 6: Departure

    Enjoy a light breakfast at the lodge. Morning airport transfers will be arranged, leaving you with full hearts, lasting memories, and a deep connection to the spirit of Timbavati.
